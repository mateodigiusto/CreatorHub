03 · Styles

Ten looks, ready to use. Or build your own.

  1. 01

    Big Caps

    Heavy white caps, one word lit, big words on the points that matter. The short-form standard.

  2. 02

    Apple UI

    Frosted glass captions, pills and number cards. Soft, app-like motion.

  3. 03

    Real Estate

    Property reels: big white caps, green numbers, glass location tags, a cinematic push.

  4. 04

    Rating

    A score card for everything you rate, and a running list of the results.

  5. 05

    Comparison

    This against that: two sides, ticks and crosses, and a winner.

  6. 06

    Podcast

    Quote captions with a speaker tag. Built for clips from long conversations.

  7. 07

    Tutorial

    Numbered steps, a progress bar and the thing you are showing, up close.

  8. 08

    Clean

    Sentence case on a soft dark box, lower third. Calm and readable.

  9. 09

    Editorial

    Serif captions and slow moves for story-led and documentary pieces.

  10. 10

    Minimal

    Small, light captions with lots of air. For quiet, premium brands.

05 · How it works

Your Claude does the work.

01

Connect Claude Code once

One line links it to your CreatorHub. It runs on your own Claude plan, so there is no extra bill.

02

Say what you want

Choose your clips or paste a link, type or say the brief, pick a style.

03

Claude edits it on your computer

With HyperFrames, which needs no editing program, or inside Premiere Pro. Your footage never leaves your machine.
