How it works

  1. Open CreatorHub on claude.ai, signed in to your Claude account.
  2. Answer four short screens: who you are, your style, your editing look, connect.
  3. Press “Connect your Claude account” once. From then on every feature runs on your own plan, like a chat message.
  4. Drop your first clip.
