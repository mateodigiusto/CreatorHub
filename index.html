AI editor

Point it at your footage and say what you want. Pick a style. Claude Code cuts it, captions it and builds the graphics on your computer, then hands you the finished video. Ask for changes in plain words.

Styles you can see

Big Caps, Apple UI, Real Estate, Rating, Comparison and more, each one moving in front of you. Or describe your own look, point it at videos you like, and save it under your name.

Performance

Bring in your numbers. Views by hook type, by format, by weekday, and a plain-words read of what to repeat.

Scripts

One topic in, a full script out, three hook options, written to sound like you and not like a model.

Competitors

Add a handle and their best posts. Their patterns, their hook types ranked by views, and five hooks adapted to your niche.

Planner

A board for what you are making and where each piece is. Type in any column to add to it.

No editing program needed

HyperFrames builds the whole video in code and renders the file. Edit in Premiere Pro? Claude works inside your project instead.

How it works

  1. Open your invite link.
  2. Connect Claude Code with one line. It runs on your own Claude plan: no API key, no extra bill.
  3. Answer three short screens: who you are, what you want, how your videos are made.
  4. Queue your first edit and say “run CreatorHub” in Claude Code.
